Privacy Policy — HKMV Tasks

Last updated 12 October 2026

HKMV Tasks is an internal task-management app for the staff and volunteers of Srila Prabhupada's Hare Krishna Movement, Visakhapatnam ("HKM Vizag", "we", "us"). It is not a general-purpose app: signing in needs an account on HKM Vizag's own ERPNext system.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes and how long it stays. It covers the Android app published as com.hkmv.hkmv_tasks.

The short version

  • The app stores the work you do in it on HKM Vizag's own ERPNext server — the same system the organisation already uses.
  • We do not sell your data, show you advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • There is no analytics or advertising software in the app.
  • The only outside service involved is Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications.

What the app collects, and why

WhatWhy
Your email address It is how you sign in, and how a task is attributed to you.
Your password Used to sign in to ERPNext. If you tick "Keep me signed in", it is stored only on your phone, in Android's encrypted keystore, so the app can renew its own session without asking you again. It is never sent anywhere except to HKM Vizag's ERPNext server to sign in.
What you write — task names, descriptions, comments, deadlines, and who you assign work to This is the content of the app. It is stored on HKM Vizag's ERPNext server and is visible to colleagues involved in that task, as it is on the website.
Photos and files you attach Only the ones you choose to attach to a task or a comment. The app asks for access to your photos or files at the moment you attach one; it does not read your gallery otherwise.
A notification token (a Firebase device ID) So a notification about your work can reach this phone. It identifies the installation, not you personally.

Kept on your phone only

  • Your sign-in session and, if you asked to stay signed in, your password — in Android's encrypted keystore.
  • Images and files you have already opened, so they do not download twice. You can clear these any time: Menu → Settings → Downloaded files.
  • Colleagues' display names, so the app does not ask the server for the same names repeatedly.

All of this is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Who your information is shared with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or any purpose unrelated to running this app. Your information reaches only:

  • HKM Vizag's ERPNext server, operated for the organisation on Frappe Cloud. This is where the work lives, and the app is simply another way to read and write it. Colleagues can see the tasks and comments they are part of, exactly as on the website.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which carries notifications to your phone. It receives the notification token and the message being delivered. Google's handling of that is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How it is protected

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • A saved password is held in Android's encrypted keystore, not in ordinary app settings, so it is not readable from a backup.
  • Your sign-in session expires, and the app signs in again quietly rather than keeping a permanent key on the device.
  • Signing out removes the session, the saved password and the files kept on the phone, and tells the server to stop sending notifications to it.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

How long it is kept

Tasks, comments and attachments stay on HKM Vizag's ERPNext server for as long as the organisation keeps them, under its own record-keeping practices — the app does not set a separate retention period. Everything held on your phone goes when you sign out or uninstall.

Your choices

  • Notifications can be turned off for the app in Android Settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Staying signed in is a choice at sign-in. Leave it unticked and no password is kept after you sign out.
  • Files kept on the phone can be cleared from Menu → Settings → Downloaded files.
  • Access, correction or deletion of your account and its data is handled by HKM Vizag's ERP administrator, since the account is an ERPNext account. Write to the address below and we will act on it, subject to the organisation's own record-keeping obligations.

Children

The app is for the staff and volunteers of HKM Vizag and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will be communicated through the organisation's usual channels.
